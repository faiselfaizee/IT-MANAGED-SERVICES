*Last updated: 19 November 2026*

This policy explains how DamazeTech Solutions (OPC) Private Limited handles personal data on deskask.in, in the DeskAsk store and in the DeskAsk service. It is written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules, and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

### 1. Two roles

- **For your account, billing and our website,** we decide why and how personal data is used. We are the data fiduciary.
- **For the data you put into DeskAsk** (tickets, assets, users, passwords, backups), your organisation decides, and we process it only on your instructions. Your organisation is the data fiduciary and answers requests about that data; we help it do so.

### 2. What we collect

| Data | Examples | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | name, work email, phone, company, role | to create and run your account, send service messages |
| Billing | company name, billing address, GSTIN, invoices, payment status | to bill you, issue GST invoices, meet tax law |
| Payment | handled by Razorpay or Stripe; we receive only the payment status and a reference, never card or bank details | to confirm payment |
| Usage and security | sign-in times, IP address, browser, audit log of actions | to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, fix problems |
| Licence activation (on-prem) | licence key reference, installation id, customer id, server name, version | to issue and renew your licence; no business data is sent |
| Support | messages and attachments you send us | to answer you |
| Website | essential cookies for sign-in and security | to make the site work; we do not use advertising cookies |

### 3. Why we may use it

We use personal data on the basis of your consent, or for the legitimate uses the law allows, such as providing a service you asked for, meeting legal obligations (for example GST records) and preventing fraud. We send product news only if you agree, and you can stop it at any time.

### 4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers who help us run DeskAsk and are bound to protect the data: payment processors (Razorpay, Stripe), our email provider, and hosting and backup providers. We also share data when the law requires it. We never sell personal data.

### 5. Where it is kept

DeskAsk SaaS data is stored on servers in India. For customers outside India, data may be processed in India. On-prem installations keep all business data on the customer's own servers.

### 6. How long we keep it

- Account and usage data: while your account is active, then up to 90 days.
- Customer Data in SaaS: deleted 90 days after the plan ends; unconverted trials, 45 days after the trial ends.
- Billing records and invoices: as long as tax and company law require (up to 8 years).
- Backups: overwritten in their normal cycle after deletion.

### 7. Security

We use encryption in transit, encrypted secrets, role-based access, two-step sign-in, audit logs and regular security testing. Vault items are encrypted in the user's browser, so we cannot read them. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

### 8. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct, complete, update or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, raise a grievance, and nominate someone to act for you. Write to servicedesk@deskask.in (subject: Privacy). For data your organisation put into DeskAsk, ask your organisation first; we will help it answer.

### 9. Children

DeskAsk is a business service and is not meant for anyone under 18.

### 10. Grievances

Write to our grievance officer (see Contact us). We acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within one month. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

### 11. Changes

We will post updates here with a new date, and email account holders about material changes.
