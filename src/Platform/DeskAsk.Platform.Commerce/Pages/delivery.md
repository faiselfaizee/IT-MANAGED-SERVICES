DeskAsk is software delivered online. Nothing is shipped, and there are no shipping charges.

| What you buy | How and when it is delivered |
| --- | --- |
| SaaS plan or free trial | Your DeskAsk address (for example yourcompany.deskask.in) and a link to set your password are emailed to you, usually within a few minutes of payment or sign-up |
| Upgrade or added module | Active in your account straight away |
| On-prem plan | A licence key is shown after payment and emailed to you; the installer is downloaded from your account. Type the key on your Licence page to activate |
| Invoice | Emailed as a PDF and kept in your account |

If you have not received your access within 2 hours of payment, check your spam folder, then write to servicedesk@deskask.in with your payment reference. We will deliver it or refund the payment in full.
