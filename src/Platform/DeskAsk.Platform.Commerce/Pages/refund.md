*Last updated: 19 November 2026*

You can cancel any DeskAsk plan at any time from Administration › Licence or by writing to servicedesk@deskask.in. A cancelled plan keeps working until the end of the period already paid for and is not renewed.

| Situation | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| Free trial | Nothing to pay, nothing to refund |
| Monthly plan, cancelled | No refund for the current month; it runs to its end |
| Yearly plan, cancelled within 7 business days of the first payment | Full refund |
| Yearly plan, cancelled after 7 business days | No refund; it runs to the end of the year |
| Renewal charged by mistake (cancelled before the renewal date, or charged twice) | Full refund of that charge |
| Upgrade during a period | The difference is charged pro rata; a downgrade takes effect at the next renewal, with no refund |
| We cannot provide the service you paid for, or we remove a core function during a paid period | Pro rata refund for the unused part |

**How refunds are paid.** Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay or Stripe within 7 business days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it. For Indian customers, a GST credit note is issued with every refund.

**After cancellation.** When a SaaS plan ends, your data stays read-only for 90 days so you can export it, and is then deleted. On-prem data stays on your own servers.

**Questions or disputes.** Write to servicedesk@deskask.in. If you are not satisfied with the answer, contact our grievance officer (see Contact us).
